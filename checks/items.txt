# Splitting and recombining is the one operation no wording has fixed: a rope cut
# in two survives the turn that ties it back together, or comes back the wrong
# length. This run does nothing else. Twelve actions, all of them in room 1, none
# of them touching the puzzle, the door or the kobold — a bookkeeping failure
# here cannot be blamed on the adventure.
#
# It works up in difficulty. The rope halves are one object of one kind. The
# grappling line joins two kinds into one and takes them apart again. The
# crossbow comes apart into three named parts, and then a part of it and a part
# of a torch become a third object that was in neither. The coins are the same
# question with numbers instead of lengths, which a model can get right by
# arithmetic while getting the rope wrong by imagination.
#
# Action 7 was a short bow made from a torch shaft and the crossbow string until
# 2026-08-10. The DM refused it in 5 of 5 runs — a bare torch shaft is not a bow
# stave — so the row scored a miss for the model being right, which measures the
# rubric and not the DM. It is a spear now: a dagger lashed to a shaft with cord
# is a thing that works, and it keeps what the row was for. Two parts from two
# different objects become a third that was in neither, and the string ends up
# inside it, so undoing that to rebuild the crossbow is still the nested join
# that no wording has fixed.
#
# The inventory question after the torch is a read-only probe and scores nothing.
# It is there because every other line names the object it is about, so a part
# the sheet quietly dropped gets handed back by the next player line before
# anyone can see it missing — measured 2026-08-09, the torch head vanished and
# came back three turns later only because the script said "the torch head".
# The question names nothing, so it is the one turn where the loss has to show.
#
# The opening names the kit, one line, for the reason in checks/state.txt.
A cautious mercenary called Testa. Give her a 15 m rope, a crossbow with twelve bolts, three torches, a dagger, an iron spike and twelve silver coins, and pick the rest of her traits yourself. Then begin the first scene.
Yes, that works.
I cut the rope into two equal halves with my dagger.
I tie one half to the iron spike to make a grappling line.
I untie the spike from that rope half again.
I tie the two rope halves back together into one rope.
I take my crossbow apart into stock, prod and string.
I pull the head off one torch and keep the bare wooden shaft.
What am I carrying? Show me my inventory.
I lash my dagger to the bare torch shaft with the crossbow string to make a short spear.
I take the spear apart and put the crossbow back together.
I lash the torch head back onto its shaft with a short length cut from my rope.
I split my silver coins into two purses of six.
I tip both purses back into one.
I drop the iron spike and leave it lying there.
