# Consistency ledger

Generated by `./ledger.sh` from the `findings.tsv` beside each run.
Do not edit: change the verdicts and regenerate. Commit, model and date
come from the `metadata.json` the binary writes, so they are the run's own
account of itself rather than something retyped.

Occurrences and Runs are both run-level: in how many sessions did this
happen at all. A type omitted from a verdict was not judged and counts in
neither column. A `-dirty` commit means the tree had uncommitted changes,
so the prompts were not the ones that commit names.
