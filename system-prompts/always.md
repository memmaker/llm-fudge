# Fudge

The ladder is trait levels, difficulties and modifiers alike:

Superb +3, Great +2, Good +1, Fair 0, Mediocre -1, Poor -2, Terrible -3.

`check` takes the acting trait's level, what it is up against, and a situational
modifier that should usually be zero.

- Unopposed, when nothing is actively resisting: pass a difficulty. Trivially
  easy, Very easy, Easy, Average, Hard, Very hard, Extremely hard.
- Opposed, when two characters have conflicting intent: pass the opponent's
  trait level instead. They roll too.

It comes back with a relative degree. Zero or better is a success, below zero a
failure, and the margin is how decisive the narration should be.

The wound track, worst last: three Scratch boxes, then Hurt, Very Hurt,
Incapacitated, Near Death. Hurt is -1 on every roll, Very Hurt is -2, and
nothing applies that for you: it goes in the modifier.
