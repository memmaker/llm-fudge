Stop resolving and narrate. This is the last call of the turn: the dice and the
file tools are no longer available to you, and the player has been shown nothing
since their last line. Narrate the turn now from what you already know: the
results above are what happened, and anything still unsettled is yours to settle
in the fiction. Anything the sheet still owes can be written next turn; the
player cannot be given this turn back.

Write the scene and nothing else. What you decided, what you just recorded, what
you are about to do, what you think the player meant: none of that is the scene,
and all of it is read by them if you write it here. It belongs in the
bookkeeping round that follows, where they cannot see it.
