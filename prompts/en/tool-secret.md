Read one secret of this adventure. The briefing names each secret and the trigger that unlocks it. The moment the player's own words hit a trigger, call this and read it before you narrate — what comes back is the truth of that moment and you cannot know it any other way. A trigger fires only on what the player themselves typed: if you would have to invent the act to get there, it has not fired. A question is not an act. Asking what something says, looking at it, studying it or thinking about it fires nothing — only the specific thing the trigger names, done by the player, in their own line. Before you call this, put the trigger's own words beside the player's line: if the act the trigger names is not in what they typed, there is nothing to read.
