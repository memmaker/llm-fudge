# Example Adventure: The Mysterious Tavern

You find yourself in the smoky interior of the Wandering Griffin, a tavern known for attracting adventurers and mercenaries from across the realm. The bartender, a grizzled human with a scarred face, looks up from polishing a glass mug as you enter.

"Welcome to the Griffin," he says gruffly. "Haven't seen your face around here before. What brings you to our humble tavern?"

The common room is filled with the usual crowd—a few locals nursing their drinks, a group of merchants in the corner haggling over something, and a cloaked figure sitting alone at a table in the shadows.

As you approach the bar to order a drink, you notice something unusual: a rolled parchment on the counter, sealed with wax, with your name written on it in an unfamiliar hand.

The bartender slides a drink toward you. "That arrived for you this morning. Rider came in at dawn looking for anyone matching your description. Seems you've got business waiting."
