The Monolith Burger orbits a dying blue star, its neon crown visible from half the system and its menu infamous in every spacer port worth a docking fee. It is neutral ground—officially—where imperial officers, rebel couriers, and freelance crews all pretend not to recognize one another over towers of synth-meat and gravity-fried rings. The air hums with languages, deals, and quiet grudges. Tonight, the place is packed, and the line for a table wraps past a holo-statue of some forgotten war hero whose eyes track every passerby.

Then, at a corner booth beneath a flickering constellation display, three slate-skinned aliens begin to convulse. Their meal—something black, glossy, and still faintly moving—spills across the table as they vomit in unison, a viscous, luminous fluid that eats into the surface like acid and releases a sweet, metallic scent. The nearest patrons recoil; others pretend not to notice. The staff freeze. For a split second, the entire restaurant holds its breath.

Security shutters slam down over the exits.

A calm, synthetic voice announces a temporary quarantine due to a "biohazard anomaly," while a squad of heavily armed corporate marshals emerges from hidden doors, sealing off sections of the dining floor. Across the room, you catch glimpses of familiar danger: an imperial attaché quietly activating a concealed comm, a rebel courier palming a data chip beneath the table, and a nervous cook trying to wipe away the glowing bile before it burns through to the deck plating—and revealing something beneath the laminate that was never meant to be seen.

As the alien fluid begins to pulse with faint, deliberate light, it sketches a pattern across the table—coordinates, or perhaps a signal—and the afflicted diners stop retching all at once. They sit upright, eyes shining with the same cold glow, and turn in perfect unison toward the center of the restaurant. Whatever just arrived in their bodies is awake, and it has chosen this crowded, fragile truce as its stage. Whether this is a weapon, a message, or the first ripple of something ancient stirring beneath the galaxy’s polished surface, the doors are sealed, the guns are out, and everyone inside is now part of it.
